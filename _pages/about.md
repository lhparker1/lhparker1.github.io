I'm a Ph.D. student in physics at UC Berkeley, working with [Uroš Seljak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uro%C5%A1_Seljak) at the Berkeley Center for Cosmological Physics and Lawrence Berkeley National Lab, and supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.

I work on cosmology, which is the study of the origin, contents and evolution of the Universe. It is a historical science: we have one Universe to observe, and we reconstruct its past from the evidence it left behind. I am broadly interested in theoretical, statistical, and observational cosmology, in particular as it relates to galaxy surveys and the open questions on dark energy. I'm a member of the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI)](https://www.desi.lbl.gov/) collaboration.

I also co-lead [Lightcone Research](https://lightconeresearch.org/) at the [Berkeley Institute for Data Science](https://bids.berkeley.edu/programs-initiatives/lightcone-research), which builds open infrastructure for rigorous AI-assisted science. Before Berkeley I was a predoctoral fellow at the Flatiron Institute's Center for Computational Astrophysics, and I studied physics at Princeton.

You can read more about my [research](/research/), browse my [code](/code/), or see my [CV](/cv/).
